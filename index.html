Why a7 studio exists

My mother Sunita stitched our school uniforms on a hand-cranked machine in Pune, and I grew up under that table picking up pins. I never forgot how good it felt to wear something made for me.

In 2025, my cousin Rohan and I took a small room, bought two machines and started a7 studio. We were tired of watching friends buy clothes that fit in the shoulders but not the waist.

We are still young, so every customer matters to us. If something is wrong, we fix it again at no charge.

Aarti Kulkarni, owner
